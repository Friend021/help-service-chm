Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I reset my password?

Go to the login screen and click Forgot Password. An email will be sent to your registered address.

Q: What browsers are supported?

The application supports Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Edge 90+, and Safari 14+. Internet Explorer is not supported.

Q: Where are log files stored?

Log files are located at %APPDATA%\MyApp\logs\ on Windows and ~/.myapp/logs/ on Linux.

Q: How do I contact support?

Open a ticket at support.example.com or email support@example.com.
